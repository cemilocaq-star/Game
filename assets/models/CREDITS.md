# Model library (CC0)

All models below are by Kay Lousberg (KayKit, https://kaylousberg.com), released under CC0 1.0 (Public Domain,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Credit is not required but given here. Copied unchanged from the
original glTF/GLB files; the original LICENSE.txt of each pack states CC0.

| Folder | Pack | Source | Models |
|---|---|---|---|
| dungeon/ | KayKit Dungeon Remastered 1.0 | https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Dungeon-Remastered-1.0 | 49 |
| space/ | KayKit Space Base Bits 1.0 | https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Space-Base-Bits-1.0 | 33 |
| prototype/ | KayKit Prototype Bits 1.0 | https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Prototype-Bits-1.0 | 23 |

Total: 105 models. Dungeon models are single .glb files with their texture embedded; space and prototype models are
.gltf + .bin plus one shared PNG per pack (spacebits_texture.png, prototypebits_texture.png).

Used by the maps in scripts/kaykit_maps.gd (Crypt Dungeon = dungeon/, Orbital Outpost = space/). The prototype/ models and
the remaining dungeon/space models are an unused library for future maps and props.
Downloaded 2026-11-27.
